Telegram channels in a clean feed. No chat noise.


==============================================================================
FULL DESCRIPTION  (max 4000 chars)
==============================================================================
Hortay turns your Telegram channels into a clean social-media feed.
No chat clutter, no "suggested" posts, no ads.

✦ A familiar social feed
Posts, photos, and reactions in one feed — without digging through hundreds
of chat messages. Put the newest on top, or at the bottom like a chat.

✦ Find anything
Search posts across all your channels, even ones that never reached your feed.
Quick filters in the Channels tab — Unread, Today, Hidden — and a one-step way
to hide or show every channel.

✦ Less repetition
A channel's burst of posts folds under its first one, and a post forwarded by
several of your channels shows up once. Turn it off in Settings if you prefer.

✦ Posts as written
Quotes, infographics, custom emojis, animated stickers, tables and formulas —
shown the way the author wrote them. Pinned messages stay one tap away, and
long posts can be summarized when Telegram offers a summary.

✦ Discover more
See similar channels Telegram recommends and subscribe in one tap.

✦ Discussions without the flood
Comments open as threads, not as an endless group chat.

✦ Keeps storage under control
A "Storage and traffic" panel shows real numbers: how much was downloaded
and how much space the cache takes. Clear it in one tap.

✦ Fast to open
Starts quickly, even with hundreds of channels.

✦ Privacy by default
No analytics, no Firebase, no ad SDKs. Internet is used only to talk to
Telegram, plus a public list of suggested channels. Open source, in 13
languages.

✦ No account? No problem
Guest mode reads public channels without signing in.

Hortay uses Telegram's official library (TDLib). Sign-in goes through the
same Telegram API used by the official client: you log in with your phone
number.

Hortay is for people who read Telegram channels more than they message.
