FULL DESCRIPTION  (max 4000 chars)
==============================================================================
Hortay turns your Telegram channels into a clean social-media feed.
No chat clutter, no "suggested" posts, no ads.

✦ Twitter-style feed
One post, photo, reactions — no wading through 800-message group queues.

✦ Posts as written
Quotes, infographics, custom emoji, animated stickers — never clipped,
no "show more" after the third sentence.

✦ Discussions, not avalanches
Comments rendered as threads, like a proper social network. Not an endless
scroll into an 8000-message group chat.

✦ Doesn't eat your storage
A "Storage and traffic" panel shows real numbers — what was downloaded,
what is taking up space. One-tap cache clear.

✦ Fast cold start
Pre-built Baseline Profiles. No jank on 200 channels.

✦ Privacy by default
No analytics, no Firebase, no ad SDKs. Internet is used only to talk to
Telegram. Open source.

Hortay uses Telegram's official library (TDLib) — your account lives in
the same API as the official client. It is not a "third party" — it is
a standard login with your phone number.

Hortay is for people who read Telegram channels more than they message.
