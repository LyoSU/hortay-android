FULL DESCRIPTION  (max 4000 chars)
==============================================================================
Hortay turns your Telegram channels into a clean social-media feed.
No chat clutter, no "suggested" posts, no ads.

✦ A familiar social feed
Posts, photos, and reactions in one feed — without digging through hundreds
of chat messages.

✦ Posts as written
Quotes, infographics, custom emojis, animated stickers — never clipped,
no "show more" after the third sentence.

✦ Discussions without the flood
Comments open as reply threads, not as an endless group chat.

✦ Keeps storage under control
A "Storage and traffic" panel shows real numbers: how much was downloaded
and how much space the cache takes. Clear it in one tap.

✦ Fast to open
Starts quickly, even with hundreds of channels.

✦ Privacy by default
No analytics, no Firebase, no ad SDKs. Internet is used only to connect to
Telegram. Open source.

Hortay uses Telegram's official library (TDLib). Sign-in goes through the
same Telegram API used by the official client: you log in with your phone
number.

Hortay is for people who read Telegram channels more than they message.
