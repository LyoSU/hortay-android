Hortay is an independent reader for Telegram channels. Every channel you follow lands in one feed, in order, with a marker where you stopped last time.

Telegram shows channels as a chat list. With ten channels that works. With two hundred you open a chat, scroll, go back, open the next one, and still don't know what you missed. Hortay turns the same channels into a timeline.

ONE FEED FOR ALL YOUR CHANNELS
• Newest first, or chat-style: read posts above, unread below, and you land on the first unread post
• A post counts as read after it stayed on screen for a moment, and the read state syncs back to Telegram
• Tabs for your Telegram folders and the archive
• Search across all channels, and a Saved tab for bookmarks

NO REPEATS, NO POST FLOODS
• A post forwarded by several of your channels shows once
• A burst of posts from one channel folds under the first one
• When several channels cover the same story, each post shows who else wrote about it

READ WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
• Guest mode reads public channels with no sign-in and no phone number
• Follows, bookmarks and read positions stay on your device

A QUIET FEED
• Hide a channel for 8 hours, a day or a week without unsubscribing
• Word filter: posts with the words or hashtags you list stay out
• Mark every channel as read in one step

POSTS THE WAY TELEGRAM SHOWS THEM
• Polls and quizzes, reactions, custom emoji, animated stickers
• Albums, inline video, round video messages, voice and audio with speed control and a mini-player
• Rich formatting: headings, tables, code blocks, formulas
• Comments as threads, pinned messages, translation, fact checks

AND ALSO
• Optional local archive of edited and deleted posts, with a diff between versions
• SOCKS5, HTTP and MTProto proxy with automatic switching
• Wallpaper or brand colours, light, dark and pure black themes, four text sizes
• Tablet and wide-window layouts
• 13 languages

PRIVACY
No analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no third-party push. Hortay talks to Telegram directly through TDLib, Telegram's official open-source client library, with no server of its own in between. You sign in through Telegram's standard authentication, and your account stays on Telegram's servers. The app is open source.

Hortay is a reader: you can react, vote in polls and read comments, but there is no message composer. It is for people who read Telegram channels more than they message.
